🌐 Community Rules

✅ Treat all members with respect.
✅ No harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
✅ No advertising other communities without permission.
✅ Do not spam channels or abuse mentions.
✅ Follow Discord and FiveM Terms of Service.
✅ Listen to Staff decisions and instructions.

🎭 Roleplay Rules

✅ Stay in character during roleplay situations.
✅ No RDM (Random Deathmatch).
✅ No VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch).
✅ No FailRP or unrealistic actions.
✅ No Metagaming (using information your character does not know).
✅ No Powergaming (forcing actions on other players).
✅ Create realistic and enjoyable roleplay scenarios.

🛡️ Staff Rules

✅ Staff must remain professional at all times.
✅ Do not abuse staff permissions.
✅ Treat every player fairly.
✅ Do not use staff powers during roleplay.
✅ Follow the chain of command.
✅ Document major punishments and decisions.

🚓 LEO Rules

✅ Follow department SOPs and guidelines.
✅ Maintain realistic police procedures.
✅ Use proper radio communication.
✅ Do not abuse law enforcement powers.
✅ Respect other departments and civilians.
✅ Supervisors must lead professionally.

🚗 Civilian Rules

✅ Civilians must create realistic roleplay.
✅ No intentionally ruining scenes.
✅ Follow traffic laws when appropriate.
✅ Respect law enforcement and emergency services.
✅ Participate in quality roleplay scenarios.
✅ No exploiting or abusing game mechanics.

⚖️ Punishment System

🟡 Warning — Minor rule violations.

🟠 Kick — Repeated issues or disruption.

🔴 Temporary Ban — Serious violations or continued rule breaking.

⚫ Permanent Ban — Severe violations, cheating, exploitation, or major community harm.
